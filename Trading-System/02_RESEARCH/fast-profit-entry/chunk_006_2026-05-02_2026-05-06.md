## Independent challenge / lessons
1. `Transition > Level` survives. BABY is a useful positive with PE6 only ~0.764, while SPACE shows PE6≈1 can be dangerous.
2. `Acceptance / price response` remains essential. PE strength nominates an EDP-like watch state; it does not establish EAP.
3. `Response-Lag Decay` strengthens: delayed expansion such as AKE should lose Fast Detach score even if eventual MFE is positive.
4. `Catastrophic-Path Veto` strengthens materially: SPACE demonstrates why MFE-only or eventual-winner labels are invalid for this objective.
5. Fast Detach should reward `Transition/Acceleration → HTF catch-up → Price Acceptance → Persistence → Fast Expansion`, while penalizing response lag and pulse/fragile path risk.
6. +5/+8 remain mandatory candidate first-profit targets, but this schema cannot provide exact TP-before-SL or Time-to-Target. Do not declare an optimal target yet.
