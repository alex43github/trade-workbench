## Independent challenge / lessons
1. **Transition > Level survives.** BABY is a better fast-detach archetype than several PE6≈1 names; absolute PE extremity is not necessary.
2. **Path Quality Gate strengthens.** Fast Detach must score MAE / path ordering separately from eventual MFE. SPACE cannot be labeled a quality success despite +28% MFE.
3. **Catastrophic-Path Veto is strongly supported.** `LiqRiskFlag`, severe MAE, and ambiguous intrabar ordering should dominate upside potential in the score.
4. **Response-Lag Decay strengthens.** CHILLGUY and AKE show that even low-MAE or eventually profitable paths can tie capital up for 15–18h; delayed response must lose score for this user's objective.
5. **Acceptance Velocity remains candidate.** BABY's 2.25h completion is qualitatively closer to the target than CHILLGUY/AKE, but literal 5m/15m acceptance timing is unavailable.
6. **+5/+8 remain explicit first-profit candidates.** AKE reaches only the medium-upside zone after a long hold; exact +5/+8/+10/+15/+20 first-hit and TP-before-SL statistics cannot be computed from this schema, so no optimal target is declared.
7. **EDP vs EAP separation remains necessary.** PE snapshots can nominate attention (EDP analogue), but the source cannot prove the lower-timeframe hand-off/acceptance needed for an actionable EAP.
