## Candidate model update

Current candidate structure survives another OOS window:

`Transition/Ignition -> Price Acceptance -> Response-qualified Early Breadth -> Response Clock -> Expansion`

with strong penalties/vetoes for:
- catastrophic/adverse MAE before useful expansion;
- stale response / long capital occupation;
- safe-but-dead low-volatility drift;
- static-PE-only ignition without price hand-off.

Candidate Response Quality remains:

`Early Expansion Velocity × Low Pre-Expansion MAE × Fresh Response Breadth × Stale-Time Decay`

This remains a candidate hypothesis, not a production trading rule.
