## Independent hypothesis challenge
1. **Transition/response > static PE level — SUPPORTED again.** Numerous PE6≈1 / strong-alignment names fail to expand quickly. Static PE remains discovery/ranking information, not EAP authorization.
2. **Acceptance / Expansion Velocity — SUPPORTED indirectly.** The three TP2 paths differ materially in speed and MAE. FHE has excellent MAE but slow response; DODOX is faster but has ~-4.2% MAE. A fast-detach score needs a joint response-quality term, not terminal MFE alone.
3. **Early Response Breadth vs Fill Breadth — Fill Breadth REJECTED as proxy again.** 48/50 fills coexist with only 3 TP2 and weighted MFE ~3.47%.
4. **Stale Response Decay — SUPPORTED.** TRADOOR, LTC, PENDLE and FHE show that low MAE/eventual upside can consume 15-27h. Score should decay nonlinearly after the action window.
5. **Safe-but-Dead penalty — SUPPORTED.** LTC is especially clean: ~-0.19% MAE but only ~+3.17% MFE in 16h.
6. **Adverse-path penalty — SUPPORTED.** DODOX reaches +10%/TP2 in 4.5h but incurs ~-4.24% MAE; it should rank below a similarly fast low-MAE path.
