4. `Catastrophic-Path Veto` remains mandatory: SYN/BTW show that eventual 7–14% MFE cannot compensate for -16% pre-expansion path.
5. Fast-detach positives in this chunk cluster around low/moderate MAE plus short HoldHours, not maximum PE. This motivates a candidate `Fresh Expansion Override`: when current-breadth/price acceptance turns sharply positive, it may override stale weak-regime state, but this must be tested in later chunks.
