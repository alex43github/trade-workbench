## Lessons / candidate model update
The OOS evidence continues to favor:

`Transition/Ignition → Price Acceptance → Response-qualified Early Breadth → Response Clock → Expansion`

with candidate response quality:

`Early Expansion Velocity × Low Pre-Expansion MAE × Fresh Response Breadth × Nonlinear Stale-Time Decay`

and hard/strong penalties for catastrophic MAE, adverse path before useful profit, pulse/fragile response, and stale low-expansion occupancy.

Chunk 023 specifically strengthens two distinctions:
1. `Good trend ≠ fast detach`: BEAT/MMT are attractive eventual paths but still ~15h events.
2. `Low risk ≠ capital efficiency`: CROSS is low-MAE and high-MFE but ~68h, so stale-time decay must become severe rather than cosmetic.
