## Independent challenge / lessons
1. **H-FD-8 Response Clock survives OOS challenge.** STX/ICP/PAXG/SYN show that a PE ignition can remain directionally non-catastrophic yet tie capital up for 14–25h. The score should decay when expansion does not arrive promptly.
2. **H-FD-9 Path Quality Gate survives strongly.** LAB demonstrates that +18% MFE is not a Fast Detach success when MAE is -27% and liquidation risk appears.
3. **Acceptance Velocity remains central.** SCR/ROBO fail quickly despite strong short PE; ICP remains broadly aligned but slow. Static PE alignment cannot replace post-ignition price response.
4. **Low MAE is necessary-ish but insufficient.** PAXG/SYN have very small MAE but poor MFE and long holds. Fast Detach must combine low MAE with Time-to-Positive and Expansion Velocity.
5. **+5/+8 remain candidate first-profit levels.** POPCAT reaches +6.7% MFE while failing to become a +10–20 fast-detach trade; exact first-hit timing and TP-before-SL remain unavailable, so no optimal target is declared.
6. **Catastrophic-Path Veto remains a hard negative feature.** LAB is the second consecutive chunk with a dramatic MFE/MAE contradiction, supporting a veto rather than a soft penalty.
7. **EDP vs EAP remains separated.** PE6 extreme/positive alignment is useful for attention, not sufficient for action. EAP still requires lower-timeframe acceptance/hand-off evidence that this schema cannot reconstruct.
