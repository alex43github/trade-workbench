The 08-07 and especially 08-09 pockets show renewed expansion, separated by a dead 08-08. However, the aggregate schema cannot determine whether response-qualified breadth expanded *before* those TP2s, so predictive lead remains DATA_GAP.

### 2. Path-Qualified Speed — strengthened

08-07 has two TP2s but also the worst daily mean MAE (-5.09%) and two liquidation-risk records in the daily summary. Therefore a day with more fast winners can simultaneously contain materially worse adverse paths. Speed cannot be rewarded without an MAE/adverse-path gate.

08-09 has three TP2s and very high mean MFE (+14.90%) but still one liquidation-risk record and mean MAE -3.71%. This is a high-opportunity regime, not evidence that indiscriminate chasing was safe.

### 3. Same-Symbol State Reset — remains candidate

The previous chunk exposed the same symbol changing from clean fast-detach to catastrophic path on the following day. Chunk 025 provides no legal lower-timeframe state-transition reconstruction to prove the reset rule, so it remains a candidate. The episodic 0/0/2/0/3 TP2 pattern nevertheless argues against carrying yesterday's state forward without fresh acceptance evidence.
