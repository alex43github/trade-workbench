## Independent OOS challenge
### 1. Response Cluster: supported, but extremely transient
The five-day TP2 sequence is 0→1→0→3→0. 08-13 is a clear response cluster (10 executions, 3 TP2, AvgMFE +9.16%, AvgMAE -2.70%, no LiqRisk), surrounded by weak/unsafe days. This supports the candidate that fast-detach opportunities arrive in short clusters, but also shows that a cluster cannot be carried forward mechanically to the next day. 08-14 immediately falls to 0 TP2 and AvgMFE +2.49%.

### 2. Path-Qualified Speed survives a strong challenge
APRUSDT 08-13 is the cleanest fast-detach exemplar in this chunk: PE6/12/24/72 ≈ 0.809/0.820/0.835/0.722, MAE -0.45%, MFE +20.16%, TP2 in 0.25h. AVAAIUSDT is also fast enough to matter (MAE -4.88%, MFE +19.93%, TP2 6.75h) but materially worse on adverse path. BRUSDT has excellent path quality (MAE -0.31%, MFE +17.73%) yet TP2 takes 14h, so it is a good trend but not the same capital-efficiency class as APR.

The opposite cases are decisive: XANUSDT 08-10 records MFE +12.78% but MAE -41.13%, LiqRisk=true and stops after ~1h; PROMUSDT 08-11 records MFE +13.21% but MAE -27.10%, LiqRisk=true and stops in 0.75h. Eventual upside cannot rescue catastrophic path. Speed/MFE rewards must be conditional on an adverse-path gate.

### 3. Static PE remains discovery, not EAP authorization
Several high short-PE names fail to expand. LDOUSDT 08-12 has PE6≈1 but MFE only +0.76% before stopping; MEWUSDT PE6≈1 but MFE +0.65%; POLYXUSDT 08-14 PE6≈1 but MFE only +0.11%. Conversely APR's exceptional 0.25h path occurs with PE6 only ~0.809. Static PE level is therefore still useful as an EDP/ranking input, not sufficient EAP authorization.

### 4. Stale-Time penalty survives
MITOUSDT reaches TP2 with MAE -1.11% and MFE +17.85%, but takes 10.5h. BR takes 14h. GENIUSUSDT has MAE only -0.45% and MFE +8.20% but occupies 24.75h; PRLUSDT has almost zero MAE (-0.02%) and MFE +9.87% but occupies 47h. These are directionally acceptable trades but poor fast-detach capital allocation. Nonlinear Stale-Time Decay remains justified.

### 5. Fill Breadth again fails as a regime proxy
08-11 and 08-13 both execute 10/10, yet TP2 differs 1 vs 3; 08-10 executes 9/10 with zero TP2. Fill breadth does not identify response-qualified breadth. The regime feature must be based on early post-EDP response quality, not merely fills.

## Lessons carried forward
1. `Response Cluster` survives, but its state is ephemeral: 0→1→0→3→0 argues for a fresh-response requirement each session/event.
2. `Path-Qualified Speed` strengthens: APR is the desired archetype; XAN/PROM show why speed or MFE without path gating is dangerous.
3. `Same-Symbol/Fresh-State Reset` remains conceptually necessary, but exact reset authorization is DATA_GAP without lower-timeframe acceptance trajectories.
4. Candidate Fast Detach structure remains: `Transition/Ignition → Price Acceptance → Response-qualified Early Breadth → Response Clock → Expansion`, with catastrophic/adverse-path veto and nonlinear stale-time decay.
