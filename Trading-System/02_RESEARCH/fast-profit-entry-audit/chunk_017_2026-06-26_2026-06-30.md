## New candidate for next independent chunk
**H-FD-ERB — Early Response Breadth, not Fill Breadth.** Within a fixed early response window after candidate detection, measure the fraction of the pool that (a) turns positive quickly, (b) holds/reclaims structure with bounded MAE, and (c) begins real price expansion. Hypothesis: this breadth measure is a better regime gate than fill count or end-of-trade mean MFE.

Secondary challenge: **Stale Response Decay should accelerate when ERB is weak**, while fresh ERB recovery may reset the clock for new signals only, not revive stale ones.

## Round1 lesson
Chunk 017 reinforces the distinction between **EDP nomination** and **EAP authorization**. Static short PE can nominate attention; the missing decision variable is early price response. A usable Fast Detach Entry Score should therefore be structurally closer to:

`Transition/Ignition -> Acceptance -> Early Response Breadth -> Response Clock -> Expansion`,

with hard/strong penalties for catastrophic MAE, stale response, safe-but-dead capital occupation, and pulse-only PE extremes.
